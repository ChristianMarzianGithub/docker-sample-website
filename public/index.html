WILD HAIR. WARM HEARTS.

Tiny trolls.
Big hair.

With bright tufts of hair, wide smiles, and wonderfully odd little faces, troll dolls have been making people smile for generations. Here’s the story behind these pocket-sized characters.

Meet the trolls ↘
01 / THE BEGINNING

A handmade gift

In 1959, Danish woodcarver Thomas Dam made a troll doll for his daughter. His little creation caught on, and the dolls became known as Dam trolls.

02 / THE LOOK

Hair with personality

The classic troll look combines a small body, a broad grin, and a tall tuft of hair. Versions come in many colors and outfits; some even have a jewel in their belly.

03 / THE CRAZE

A collectible comeback

Troll dolls became a toy craze in the United States in the early 1960s, then enjoyed another wave of popularity in the 1990s. Collectors still seek out vintage examples today.

A LITTLE MORE TROLL LORE

Did you know?

Troll dolls are often called “good luck trolls.” Their lucky reputation is part of their playful appeal.

START YOUR OWN LITTLE COLLECTION

Choose a troll that makes you smile.

Look for a hair color or outfit you love. If you’re buying a vintage doll, check its maker’s marks and condition: dolls from different makers can look alike, and age alone doesn’t guarantee value. Keep your trolls away from direct sunlight to help protect their colors.
